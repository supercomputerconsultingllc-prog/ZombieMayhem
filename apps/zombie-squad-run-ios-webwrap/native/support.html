Help and support

Drag the battlefield or thumb pad to move. Your squad fires automatically at threats ahead. Recruit survivors for a capped fire-rate bonus. Change weapons and formations through Squad Command. Use Overdrive when surrounded.

Obstacles and supplies

Wrecks block shots and movement. Fuel drums explode when destroyed. Fire damages your squad; water slows it. Collect ammunition cases, armor supplies, rescue beacons and energy cells to improve your run.

Recovery

Use Continue Run after an interruption. Combat remains paused until you choose to resume. Save Tools can export your permanent progression and settings. Keep exported backups private.

Report a problem

Include the game version, iPhone model, iOS version, mode, run seed and what happened. Do not post passwords or exported saves publicly.

Open the project support tracker

The support tracker requires an internet connection and may require a GitHub account. Reports there are public.
