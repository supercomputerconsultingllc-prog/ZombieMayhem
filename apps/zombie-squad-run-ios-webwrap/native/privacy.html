Privacy

Zombie Mayhem's offline release does not require an account, include advertising, track you across apps, or transmit gameplay analytics to the developer.

Your progression, settings and interrupted run are saved on your device. Exported backups remain under your control. Native saved files may be included in your device backups according to your Apple settings.

Apple processes the app purchase and may provide platform diagnostics under your device settings. No payment details are collected by the game. If you voluntarily contact support or share diagnostics, the information you send is used to investigate your request.

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Restoring a device backup can restore saved data. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your progress.

This policy covers the offline build only. Any future online features will require updated disclosures.
